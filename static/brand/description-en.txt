A professional size guide on every product page

Dalilak Plus adds a "Size Guide" button to your product pages, so your customers see a clear chart in Arabic or English and choose their size with confidence. Fewer pre-purchase questions, fewer size-related returns.

What your customers get
Product measurements and body measurements in one window, with two tabs.
Instant switching between centimeters and inches, and between size systems (US, UK, EU, FR, IT, DE, AU, JP) with approximate conversion.
An illustrated "how to measure" guide and a fit scale (slim, regular, oversized).
A calculator that suggests the closest size to the customer's own measurements.

What you get as a merchant
A design that matches your brand: ready-made looks, colors, Google fonts, and color any cell with a click.
Edit the chart directly by clicking inside the live preview.
Import your chart from Excel, or from an image you own the rights to using AI, and review it before publishing.
One chart for many products or a whole category: edit it once and every linked product updates.
16 button designs, many placements, and show it on mobile or desktop only.
Insights: how often the chart is opened, which products use it most, and which sizes sell and get returned the most from your orders.

Privacy and security
It never edits your products and never stores your customers' personal data. It only reads products and orders.
